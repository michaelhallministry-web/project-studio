01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE MUSIC

Meet Michael Hall.

I'm a keyboardist, composer, and music producer whose creative path began on stage and continues in the studio. My background includes touring as a performing keyboardist in the 1990s and years of arranging, playing, and leading music.

TrackLab brings that experience together in one place: a personal studio for original instrumental work, thoughtful production, and collaboration with other musicians and creative teams.

Whether I'm developing an original composition, arranging music, or collaborating with other musicians and producers, my goal is to bring creativity, musical experience, and attention to detail to every project.
