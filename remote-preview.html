The experience

Real collaboration. Separate locations.

Remote sessions are designed to bring musicians and creative ideas together without everyone needing to be in the same room.

01 / CONNECT

Talk through your idea

We start with your music, your goals, and the sound you're trying to achieve.

02 / COLLABORATE

Work together remotely

With a compatible setup and a reliable connection, we can communicate and work through a recording session in real time.

03 / CREATE

Capture the performance

Build toward usable recordings and musical parts that serve the project—not the technology.

Behind the connection

Built around the music.

TrackLab uses Steinberg's VST Connect Pro workflow for compatible remote recording sessions. Cubase Pro is part of the studio-side setup; the goal for collaborators is a straightforward musical experience.

Before scheduling, we'll confirm the technical requirements, monitoring approach, and whether a remote session is the right fit for your project.

Remote Sessions is a proposed service page. Session availability, supported configurations, and booking details will be confirmed before public launch.
